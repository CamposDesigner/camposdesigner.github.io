Project Overview

A seasonal publication experience designed with product visibility and editorial pacing.

The Summer Guide was developed to present seasonal content in a clean and engaging format, balancing product communication, visual rhythm, and structured page composition.

The layouts focus on readability, clear hierarchy, and an organized visual system that allows each page to feel connected while maintaining its own editorial presence.

Design Direction

Seasonal storytelling supported by structure, hierarchy, and visual rhythm.

The design direction emphasized clean product presentation, balanced spacing, and a consistent editorial flow across the full guide.
