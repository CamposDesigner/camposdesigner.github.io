Project Overview

A seasonal editorial guide designed for clarity, rhythm, and product-focused storytelling.

The Christmas Guide was developed as a compact editorial experience, using typography, image hierarchy, and structured layout systems to present seasonal content in a polished and accessible way.

The project focused on creating a balanced relationship between product presentation, festive visual tone, and clear reading flow across all pages.

Design Direction

A clean seasonal layout system built around product visibility and editorial rhythm.

The design direction emphasized clear hierarchy, balanced spacing, and a refined seasonal tone to make the guide feel organized, festive, and visually engaging.
