Project Overview

A travel-oriented editorial experience designed through typography, imagery, and visual pacing.

Especial America explored how publication design could create a more immersive travel storytelling experience through structured layouts, visual hierarchy, and strong photographic sequencing.

The project focused on balancing editorial rhythm, destination imagery, readability, and publication flow to support a more engaging narrative experience.

Design Direction

Photography and typography working together through editorial travel storytelling.

The design direction emphasized visual immersion, structured pacing, and image-led narrative flow across the publication experience.
