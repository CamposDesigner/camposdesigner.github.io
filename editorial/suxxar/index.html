Project Overview

An editorial system developed around image hierarchy and publication rhythm.

The Suxxar project explored how typography, image composition, and page structure could work together to support a more immersive editorial experience.

The layouts emphasized balance, readability, and visual flow across multiple spreads while maintaining a strong branded publication identity.

Design Direction

Typography and photography working together through editorial pacing.

The design direction focused on creating a cleaner publication rhythm where imagery, typography, and page composition support a visually immersive reading experience.
