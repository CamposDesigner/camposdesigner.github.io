Project Context

Supporting global communication through consistent, adaptable visual systems.

The work focused on creating clear and engaging communication materials for a global nonprofit organization, balancing brand consistency with the flexibility needed for campaigns, publications, events, and digital outreach.

01

Challenge

Communicate across audiences and formats.

Rotary needed materials that could serve different communication goals, from publications and campaign assets to event materials and social content, while staying aligned with a recognizable global brand.

02

Approach

Create clarity through structured design.

I used editorial hierarchy, consistent typography, adaptable layouts, and strong visual organization to make complex information easier to scan, understand, and repurpose across multiple channels.

03

Outcome

A cohesive visual language across touchpoints.

The final materials supported clearer communication across print and digital formats, helping campaigns, publications, events, and social assets feel connected within the broader Rotary brand system.
