Kemper

Brochures

Turning complex corporate information into clear, polished, and scalable visual communication. These brochure systems were built to make dense business content feel organized, credible, and easy to navigate — balancing brand consistency, editorial rhythm, image hierarchy, and production-ready layouts across multiple formats.

01 Challenge

Scale without losing consistency.

Kemper needed communication materials that could support different business units, campaign needs, internal messaging, and customer-facing content while still feeling connected to the same brand system.

02 Approach

Build modular visual structures.

I used repeatable grids, consistent typography, adaptable image areas, and flexible layout patterns that could work across brochures, reports, campaigns, social assets, wireframes, and web pages.

03 Outcome

A more cohesive brand experience.

The final work created a cleaner and more scalable communication system, helping different materials feel unified while still allowing each format to serve its specific purpose.
