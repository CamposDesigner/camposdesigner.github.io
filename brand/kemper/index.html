Project Context

Creating a flexible communication system for a large corporate brand.

The work focused on building visual consistency across multiple communication needs while keeping layouts clear, adaptable, and production-ready across print and digital formats.

01

Challenge

Scale without losing consistency.

Kemper needed communication materials that could support different business units, campaign needs, internal messaging, and customer-facing content while still feeling connected to the same brand system.

02

Approach

Build modular visual structures.

I used repeatable grids, consistent typography, adaptable image areas, and flexible layout patterns that could work across brochures, posters, campaigns, social assets, wireframes, and web pages.

03

Outcome

A more cohesive brand experience.

The final work created a cleaner and more scalable communication system, helping different materials feel unified while still allowing each format to serve its specific purpose.
