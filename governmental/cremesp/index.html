CREMESP

The Regional Council of Medicine of the State of São Paulo (Cremesp) is a non-profit federal autarchy, founded on September 30, 1957. Cremesp works for the benefit of society, in the supervision of medical professional ethics through actions. The campaign for the 2011 Cremesp Exam was to work for us at Agencia Ponto F under the direction of Designer Luciano Campos. In 2011, Cremesp had the highest number of instructors for the Exam. Cremesp’s annual exam was created in favor of the best practice of medicine, and aims to ascertain the quality of the teaching given in the colleges.
