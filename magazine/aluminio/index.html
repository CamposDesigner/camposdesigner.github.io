Project Overview

A magazine system designed through visual rhythm and editorial sequencing.

Alquimia Magazine was developed as a publication experience where typography, imagery, and page structure work together to create a cohesive editorial rhythm.

The layout direction focused on readability, visual contrast, structured pacing, and the relationship between single pages and full spreads.

Design Direction

Editorial pacing built through typography, image scale, and page rhythm.

The project focused on creating a cohesive magazine experience where each spread contributes to the overall narrative flow while maintaining clarity and visual sophistication.
