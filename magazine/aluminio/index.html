Publication Design

Alquimia Magazine Layout

Editorial publication design focused on typography, visual rhythm, and immersive reading experiences.

Category

Editorial Design

Focus

Typography & Layout

Role

Art Direction

Context

Designing editorial systems that support storytelling and readability.

This project explored clean editorial pacing through typography, image balance, whitespace, and structured layout composition.

The objective was to create a visually engaging reading experience while preserving clarity and publication consistency.

Outcome

A visually structured publication experience built around typography and rhythm.

The final editorial system created a clean and immersive reading experience while supporting the visual identity of the publication.
