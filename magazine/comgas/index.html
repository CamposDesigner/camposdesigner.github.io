Magazine Case Study

Comgás Magazine

Editorial design and art direction for a branded magazine experience created for Comgás, one of the largest piped natural gas distributors in Brazil.

Client

Comgás

Publication

KAZA Magazine

Role

Creative Direction / Editorial Design

Context

Designing a branded editorial experience around lifestyle, comfort, and innovation.

Comgás has been present in Brazil for more than 140 years, serving customers across São Paulo, Campinas, Baixada Santista, and Vale do Paraíba.

The magazine translated the brand’s presence into a lifestyle-oriented editorial experience, balancing information, visual rhythm, and premium publication design.

Outcome

A premium editorial system connecting brand communication with magazine storytelling.

The final design created a visually engaging publication experience, using photography, typography, structure, and editorial pacing to communicate the Comgás brand through a lifestyle lens.
