Project Overview

A branded editorial feature designed with clarity, pacing, and product-focused visual storytelling.

The Tramontina editorial project focused on presenting brand content through a premium publication format, combining product imagery, lifestyle communication, typography, and structured page composition.

The layouts were developed to create a consistent editorial rhythm while supporting product visibility, readability, and a refined visual experience across the full sequence of pages.

Design Direction

Product imagery and editorial rhythm shaped into a refined publication experience.

The design direction focused on balancing product visibility, lifestyle storytelling, and visual consistency through careful spacing, typographic hierarchy, and page sequencing.
